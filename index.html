Frequently Asked Pressure Washing Questions

How often do I need exterior cleaning services?

It depends which services you're getting and your specific conditions. As a general guideline we recommend concrete cleaning and house washing once per year, and gutter cleaning at least once per year — though if you have a lot of trees around the house you may need it more often. We can assess your property and recommend a maintenance plan that keeps it looking its best year-round.

Are your pressure washing services eco friendly?

Yes. The equipment and cleaning solutions we use are safe for the environment, your home, and the surrounding plants and landscaping. We use biodegradable solutions and minimise water usage. Most of our work is soft washing rather than high-pressure washing — gentle pressure with effective cleaning solutions does the job.

Can pressure washing damage my property?

It can, if it isn't done correctly by a trained professional — especially on siding. We only use high pressure on hard, durable surfaces like concrete and stone. For more fragile surfaces like decks, fencing and siding we never use high pressure. Working with a professional soft washing team is how you avoid that damage entirely.

Do I need to be home during the service?

No. We never need to go inside your home, though you're more than welcome to oversee the work. Many customers prefer to book while they're at work or running errands. As long as we have access to the perimeter of the house and an outside water source, we're all set.
