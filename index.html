How often should my house be washed?

Most homes look their best on a once-a-year cycle. Shaded or wooded lots with heavy tree cover tend to green up faster and do better every 8–10 months, while sunnier exposures can stretch to 18 months.

Will pressure washing damage my siding or roof?

Not the way we do it. Siding, stucco, painted wood and shingles all get a low-pressure soft wash — the detergent does the work and we rinse at roughly garden-hose pressure. High pressure is reserved for concrete, brick and pavers.

Are the detergents safe around plants and pets?

We pre-wet and post-rinse all landscaping, use tarps where needed, and keep animals inside until we've done the final rinse. Once everything is dry, the yard is completely safe to use again.

Do I need to be home?

Nope. As long as we can reach an outside spigot and the gates are unlocked, you can be at work. We'll text before we start and send photos when we're done.

What does it cost?

It depends on square footage, height and how much buildup there is — which is why we quote rather than list prices. Most single-family house washes land in a predictable range and we'll tell you the exact number before we touch anything.

What if I'm not happy with the result?

We come back and redo it at no charge. That's the whole guarantee — no forms, no argument. It's happened rarely, and every time we fixed it same week.
